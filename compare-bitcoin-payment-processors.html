How to use this comparison

1. Start with your existing stack

If your POS already supports Bitcoin natively, that may reduce operational change. If it does not, evaluate a browser terminal or separate payment layer before replacing hardware.

2. Decide what you want to receive

Some merchants want BTC. Others want dollars or another fiat currency. Settlement preference can eliminate options quickly.

3. Model the whole cost

Processing rate is only one component. Include conversion, payout, network and operational costs where they apply.

Commercial relationships

Speed, CoinGate and NOWPayments publicly offer merchant referral or affiliate programs. Bitcoin4Retail may participate in payment-provider affiliate programs as relationships are established. Compensation is disclosed and does not change the comparison framework.

Why this site exists

Merchants should not have to become Bitcoin experts—or replace a working POS—to make a sound payment decision. Our job is to translate the fragmented provider market into business terms.
