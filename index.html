There is no single “Bitcoin POS.” There are different ways to get paid.

A Square merchant can use native Bitcoin acceptance. A restaurant on a legacy POS may add a browser-based Lightning terminal. An ecommerce seller may prefer a gateway or plugin. We compare those paths on the same merchant-first framework.

Proof in the real world

How Steak ’n Shake rolled Bitcoin out nationwide.

Steak ’n Shake used Speed’s Lightning infrastructure across kiosks, drive-throughs and other ordering channels at 393 U.S. locations. The implementation is a useful model for how Bitcoin can be layered into existing restaurant technology.

Read the case studyReview Speed

Why this case matters

Kiosk integration. Lightning QR inside self-service.

Drive-through. Browser-based Bitcoin POS without dedicated new hardware.

Reported economics. Steak ’n Shake’s COO reported a 50% reduction in processing fees versus cards in this deployment.

Company-reported results; outcomes will vary by merchant.
