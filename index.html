Our approach

Business first. Bitcoin second.

We focus on owners and operators who are already willing to evaluate Bitcoin on the merits. The question is not whether Bitcoin is interesting. The question is whether it can reduce costs, reduce friction, improve payment operations or help attract customers for your specific business.

Our editorial standard

We lead with measurable operating outcomes, disclose meaningful limitations, separate facts from interpretation, and link back to primary Square or government sources whenever the details matter.
