There is no single “Bitcoin POS.” There are different ways to get paid.

A Square merchant can use native Bitcoin acceptance. A restaurant on a legacy POS may add a browser-based Lightning terminal. An ecommerce seller may prefer a gateway or plugin. We compare those paths on the same merchant-first framework.
