NOWPayments: flexible crypto checkout for online merchants.

NOWPayments is worth evaluating when broad asset support, API/plugin flexibility and wallet-oriented settlement matter more than a native physical-POS experience.

Without conversion1% + network fees
Converted/fixed rate1.5% + network fees
Primary orientationOnline gateway/API

Merchant fit

NOWPayments provides payment APIs, plugins, payment links and custody/wallet flows for accepting Bitcoin and other supported cryptocurrencies. It is a stronger natural fit for ecommerce and digital businesses than for a restaurant seeking a native counter POS.

Pricing

NOWPayments currently publishes a 1% service fee for payments without conversion and 1.5% for multi-currency, automatic-conversion or fixed-rate payment flows, plus applicable network fees.

Affiliate program

NOWPayments publicly advertises an affiliate program paying 25% of NOWPayments' profit from commissions generated by referred merchants. Its current help documentation says the standard reward period is five years with potential extensions for top affiliates.

Sources checked September 25, 2026: NOWPayments pricing and affiliate program.
