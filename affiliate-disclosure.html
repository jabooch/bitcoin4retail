How Bitcoin4Retail separates commercial relationships from product comparisons.

How the site may earn revenue

Bitcoin4Retail may participate in affiliate or referral programs offered by Bitcoin and crypto payment providers. When a reader uses a tracked link and completes an eligible action, Bitcoin4Retail may receive compensation at no additional cost to the reader.

Not every provider link is an affiliate link

Some provider links are ordinary direct links. Commercial relationships are added only after the relevant program is established and tracked links are available. We do not describe an ordinary link as an affiliate relationship.

Multiple providers

The site is intentionally provider-neutral in structure. Square, Speed, CoinGate, NOWPayments and other payment products may be covered whether or not Bitcoin4Retail has a commercial relationship with them.

Editorial independence

Affiliate economics do not determine our factual comparison criteria. We compare products on published pricing, settlement options, payment rails, implementation requirements, POS or ecommerce fit, merchant workflow and material limitations.

Current commercial status

Bitcoin4Retail is building relationships across multiple payment providers. Until a provider link is specifically identified as a tracked affiliate or referral link, readers should treat it as a normal provider link.
