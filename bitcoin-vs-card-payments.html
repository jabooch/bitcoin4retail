Bitcoin payments vs card payments

A practical merchant comparison of Bitcoin and card payments across fees, settlement, reversibility and checkout experience.

FactorBitcoin with SquareCards with Square
Processing fee0% through 2026; announced 1% afterVaries by plan and transaction type
Settlement railBitcoin LightningCard networks
ReversibilityFinal payment; merchant handles issues directlyChargeback/dispute process available
Customer requirementCompatible Bitcoin walletCard or compatible wallet

The right question

Bitcoin does not need to replace cards to be useful. A retailer can treat it as another payment rail and measure whether the economics and customer experience justify keeping it.

Where Bitcoin can be attractive

  • Lower processing cost for eligible payments.
  • Fast payment confirmation.
  • No traditional card chargeback flow.
  • Choice to settle in dollars or Bitcoin.

Where cards remain stronger

  • Much broader customer adoption.
  • Established consumer dispute protections.
  • Familiarity for staff and customers.
