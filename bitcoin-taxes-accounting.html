Bitcoin taxes and accounting for merchants

A practical overview of reporting and accounting questions merchants should consider before accepting Bitcoin.

Important: this page is educational information, not tax, accounting or legal advice. Rules depend on jurisdiction and business circumstances.

Start with the transaction record

Square reports Bitcoin-related transaction activity inside its commerce environment, which can help retailers reconcile sales with the rest of their Square reporting.

USD settlement can simplify the first test

A merchant that accepts Bitcoin but settles in dollars can separate the payment-method decision from the treasury decision to hold Bitcoin.

Holding Bitcoin adds another layer

If the business receives or retains Bitcoin, ask a qualified tax professional how basis, gains or losses, custody and recordkeeping apply to your entity and jurisdiction.

Operational checklist

  • Confirm how sales are recorded in your books.
  • Save Square transaction exports and receipts.
  • Document whether settlement is USD or BTC.
  • Review refund handling with your bookkeeper.
  • Get professional advice before treating Bitcoin as a treasury asset.
