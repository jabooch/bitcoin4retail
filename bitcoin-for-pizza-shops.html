Bitcoin for pizza shops

Can Bitcoin improve the economics of your next pizza order?

Pizza shops combine steady local demand, repeat customers, takeout volume and meaningful average tickets. That creates a clear environment for testing a new payment option on the numbers.

Explore Square for pizza shopsCompare payment costs
Why it matters

Bitcoin has to make the business better.

A pizza shop does not need a crypto strategy. It needs payment methods that work during a Friday night rush, fit takeout and counter service, and do not create a second operating system for the staff.

Keep more of each sale

Compare the cost of accepting Bitcoin with the payment methods you already use. The goal is better unit economics, not novelty.

Reduce payment friction

Use Bitcoin inside a familiar Square commerce workflow instead of adding a disconnected crypto checkout system.

Get paid efficiently

Evaluate settlement speed and operating cash flow alongside processing cost, customer experience and reporting.

Add customer choice

Give Bitcoin customers another way to pay without requiring every customer—or every sale—to use Bitcoin.

For this business

What the Bitcoin case looks like here.

Takeout-friendly checkout

Bitcoin acceptance should work as a payment option inside the existing order flow, not as a separate process your team has to manage.

Meaningful order sizes

Family orders, catering and group purchases can make the processing cost of a transaction easier to see in dollars.

Local loyalty

Bitcoin acceptance can be another reason for a payment-motivated local customer to choose your shop and come back.

Start practical

You do not need to rebuild your business around Bitcoin.

Start with a few measurable questions: what does it cost you to get paid today, how many customers might actually use Bitcoin, how does the checkout fit your operation, and what happens if adoption grows from a small percentage of sales?
