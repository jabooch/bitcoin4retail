What Square hardware do you need for Bitcoin?

Which Square hardware supports Bitcoin checkout and when a merchant actually needs new equipment.

You may not need new hardware

Do not buy equipment simply because Bitcoin feels new. The right hardware depends on the checkout experience you want.

Square Register (2nd generation)

This is the most distinctive Bitcoin checkout experience: customers can initiate Bitcoin from the buyer-facing display and use supported NFC Lightning wallets.

Other supported Square devices

Eligible sellers may use seller-initiated QR workflows on supported Square point-of-sale configurations. Confirm current compatibility in Square’s documentation before purchasing equipment.

Decision rule: buy hardware because it improves the whole checkout operation, not solely to add a Bitcoin logo at the counter.
