Editorial policy

How Bitcoin4Retail researches, writes, verifies and updates merchant guidance.

Merchant-first standard

We prioritize concrete business outcomes: processing cost, settlement speed, implementation complexity, customer experience, reporting and operational limitations.

Primary sources first

Product claims are checked against primary Square documentation whenever possible. Tax and regulatory topics are treated cautiously and point readers toward qualified professional advice.

No hidden catches

We disclose transaction limits, eligibility restrictions, refund differences and other material limitations even when those facts make a product less attractive.

Commercial relationships

Affiliate relationships may influence which products receive coverage, but they do not change the factual standard used to describe products, limitations and pricing.

Updates

Pages include review dates where relevant. Product details can change, so readers should verify current terms with the provider before making a business decision.
