Why Bitcoin4Retail exists and who it is built for.

Our point of view

Bitcoin4Retail helps retail owners and operators evaluate Bitcoin payments using the same standards they apply to any operating decision: economics, friction, reliability and fit.

Who we write for

Our launch audience is the retailer already open to Bitcoin and looking for a practical implementation path. We focus on useful operating questions rather than price speculation or ideology.

How we plan to make money

Bitcoin4Retail intends to earn revenue through clearly disclosed affiliate relationships with commerce platforms and products we cover. At launch, Square links are ordinary direct links while we apply to Square’s affiliate program.
