About

The decision layer for merchants who want to accept Bitcoin.

Bitcoin4Retail translates Bitcoin payment products into the things operators actually care about: cost, checkout flow, settlement, implementation and fit.

Our point of view

A merchant should not need to become a crypto expert—or replace a working commerce stack—to understand whether Bitcoin payments make business sense. We compare native POS support, add-on payment layers and online gateways using a consistent merchant-first framework.

Who we write for

We write for restaurants, coffee shops, pizza shops, retailers and online businesses that are already willing to evaluate Bitcoin and need a practical path from interest to implementation.

What we do not do

Bitcoin4Retail is not a Bitcoin price site, trading publication or ideological project. Our subject is commerce: getting paid, settlement, fees, operations and customer experience.

How we make money

Bitcoin4Retail may earn revenue through clearly disclosed affiliate or referral relationships with payment providers. The site is designed to support multiple providers because different merchants need different payment solutions.
